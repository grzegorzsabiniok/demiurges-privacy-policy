Demiurges

Privacy Policy

Developer: Berih  ·  Last updated: September 23, 2026

In short: Demiurges stores game progress on your device. Multiplayer is optional. When you use it, Epic Online Services processes a pseudonymous device-based player identifier and the information needed to run a match. We do not sell personal data or show advertisements in the current release.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Berih ("we", "us") handles information in the Demiurges game ("Demiurges" or the "Game"), including its Android version. It applies to information processed when you play the Game, whether offline or through its optional multiplayer features.

2. Information we process

Local game data. Demiurges stores gameplay progress, game settings, selected language, decks, unlocked content, play-time information, and any player name you enter in the Game's private app storage on your device. This information is used to provide the Game and restore your progress. In single-player play, it is not sent to us.

Optional multiplayer data. If you choose to use multiplayer, the Game uses Epic Online Services ("EOS"). To provide login, lobbies, matchmaking, and connections, EOS may process:

  • a pseudonymous device-based identifier and an EOS Product User ID;
  • your device model and technical connection information, including IP-address-related network information needed to establish and protect a connection;
  • the player name or display name you choose, lobby information, match status, and gameplay data needed to synchronize a multiplayer match.

Your display name and the gameplay information necessary for a match may be visible to other players in the same lobby or match. Please do not use a real name or other personal information as your display name.

Data we do not request. The current release does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos or media, SMS, or call logs. It does not require you to create an account with Berih, and it does not ask for your email address or payment-card information.

3. Why we use this information

We use the information above only to provide and operate the Game: saving progress locally, applying your settings, enabling multiplayer, preventing abuse and technical failures, and responding to privacy requests. We do not sell personal information or use it for interest-based advertising.

4. When information is shared

We share information only as necessary to operate the features you choose:

  • Epic Online Services: provides the optional anonymous login, multiplayer, lobby, and connection services. Its processing is governed by the Epic Games Privacy Policy.
  • Other players: receive your chosen display name, lobby presence, and the gameplay data necessary to participate in the same multiplayer match.
  • Legal and security purposes: information may be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the Game, its players, or our rights.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. We do not rent or sell personal information.

5. Advertising, analytics, and payments

The current release does not display advertisements, process payments, or send analytics events. The project contains legacy configuration for advertising and analytics services, but those features are not initialized by the current Game code. If a future release enables advertising, analytics, purchases, or other data-processing features, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure before that release is made available.

6. Retention and deletion

Local save data remains on your device until you delete it by clearing Demiurges' app storage or uninstalling the Game. We do not maintain a Berih-operated cloud account database for this Game.

Information processed by EOS is retained according to Epic's applicable policies and service requirements. To request deletion of data we control, or to ask us to facilitate a request concerning EOS data, contact us as described below. We may retain limited information where required to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce our rights.

7. Security

We limit local game data to the Game's app storage and use the security measures provided by the platforms and services that operate the Game. Multiplayer communications rely on EOS and its security controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the Game's features.

8. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us so we can review the request.

9. Your privacy choices and contact

You can play offline without using the multiplayer feature. You can remove local data at any time by clearing the Game's app storage or uninstalling it.

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or other inquiries, use the developer support email displayed on the Google Play listing for Demiurges and include “Demiurges Privacy” in the subject line. This is our privacy contact mechanism.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or applicable requirements change. We will post the updated version at the same public URL and revise the “Last updated” date. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in the Game and in Google Play disclosures where required.

This page is intended to be published as a publicly accessible, non-editable web page and linked both from the Game and its Google Play listing.
